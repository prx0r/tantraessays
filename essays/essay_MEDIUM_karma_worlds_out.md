## 2. Dying observed, then Yajnavalkya's whisper

Dying as lab: "what happens to a person when they die is a major theme in the early Upanishads. The process of dying itself is examined in a lot of detail." [karma-02]. Then the secret: "he takes him to one side where no one can hear. And he says to the man, he says, the person is reborn according to his good deeds in a good situation and in the bad deeds in a bad one." [karma-03]. First incarnation doctrine in Sanskrit, delivered as controversy.

Outside Vedic culture: "this notion of reincarnation, that is determined by the actions we perform in this life, comes from outside Vedic culture." [karma-04]. Sramana milieu, 120 sects, elements theory.

## 3. Images that stick

Caterpillar: "He talks about the caterpillar, and it climbs, crawls up to the top of the blade of grass... then you form into a chrysalis, as it were, the dying process... from that would be born another body, which is the butterfly." [karma-05, 00:19:02-->00:19:10]. Positive, not dread.

Yama story: "Yami was in love with Yama... because he was the first person to die, he became the Lord of Death." [karma-06]. Night/day created to abate grief, golden bridge Hiranyasetu, judge under kadamba with flute. Later file-culture: Chitragupta, Tibetan mirror.

Door password: "you go to the door and you knock on the door... he asks, who are you?... the right answer is, I am he... I am you, I am Brahma Smi. Then the door opens." [karma-07, 00:33:28-->00:34:28]. Rumi parallel noted.

## 4. Law, ghosts, and what karma is not

Mimamsa: "in Kumarila Bhatta, in Prabhakara Mimamsa, the only kind of action to which there is a consequence is ritual action. And that generates what's called Apoorva." [karma-08]. Ritual tech vs moral law; niyati fixes fruit to act.

Fieldwork: "in the first period of 11 days, then the being you're addressing to is a ghost, is a preta, is a bhuta. So you need priests, ritual specialists, who deal with ghosts." [karma-09]. Mahabrahmana feeder, Preta-kalpa reader, 30 ghost varieties, Teli oil-presser skull, Brahmin ghost worst, Madagascar/Nuer parallels, Sudhir Kakar uncle case. ## 5. The machine out

Hook: "he imagines that as he exhales in his prana there's a little golden hook..." [karma-10]. Thread: "He would then connect your Prana with his... exhale onto a thread with five knots..." [vyoma-04]. Travel: "resonant with that world... breathe you into the next one..." [vyoma-05]. Numbers: 81 parts over 118 worlds and 36 tattvas [vyoma-08]. Burn thread knot by knot, karma lived out [karma-11].

Where and why here: "it's only here... Jambudvipa... Karma Bhoomi. Because... this is where you can act..." [karma-12]. Retention: "the Guru would keep a certain amount of karma... Prarabda..." [vyoma-07].

Impurity theory: "initiation... is the removal of impurity and with that fundamental impurity is removed karma and maya... beyond Maya... pure reality levels... tiny latent impression... all that separates you from finally attaining Shiva." [karma-13]. Kashmiri nondual gloss: impurity = objectivity/difference, lack of free consciousness.

Seed gloss so mantra makes sense: Sau pervades, Au is will/knowledge/action [elucidation-02, 00:02:07-->00:02:32].
