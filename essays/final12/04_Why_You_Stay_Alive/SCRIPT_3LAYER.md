# Essay 4 — 3-layer script (primary → Mark → plain)

## Beat 1 — prarabdha kept back
PRIMARY (doctrine ref): Siddhanta prarabdha-karma — fruit already ripening, must be undergone.
MARK [Vyomavyapin_mantra.mp3 00:17:24-->00:17:35]: "So when you're initiated, the Guru would keep a certain amount of karma he wouldn't remove, it's called Prarabda, this karma which is on the way of working itself out."
PLAIN: The teacher burns your karma but leaves the portion already ripening — so you stay alive to finish this life instead of dropping dead pure.

## Beat 2 — root removed
PRIMARY (ref): Siddhanta anava/karma/mayi mala complex (cite paddhati; Sanskrit via Muktabodha, no full English to quote).
MARK [Karma_and_rebirth.mp3 ~01:08:40-->01:10:00, txt 307-309]: "Now initiation, now that fundamental impurity, from that is generated both the karma and the world and body and so on, in which that karma is made or exhausted."
PLAIN: Initiation removes the root impurity — and with it, the karma and the world-body it generates. What is kept back is the remainder already in motion.

## Beat 3 — faint trace
PRIMARY: same mala complex; pure levels beyond Maya (no PD English gloss to quote — paraphrase only).
MARK [Karma_and_rebirth.mp3 ~01:10:00-->01:12:00, txt 313-320]: "And then beyond that there are pure reality levels, in which there is no karma anymore, and there's no Maya anymore, but there's some tiny residue, a latent impression of the impurity, the fundamental impurity." + "and what remains then are just a tiny latent impression, and that tiny latent impression is all that separates you from finally attaining Shiva."
PLAIN: Past Maya, past action: pure levels with no karma, only a faint trace of impurity left. That trace is the whole distance.

## Beat 4 — accretion, most after death
PRIMARY (ref): Videhamukti vs jivanmukti distinction (Siddhanta; cite).
MARK [Vyomavyapin_mantra.mp3 00:10:30-->00:10:52]: "By accretion. And he would also accept that some people may not develop that far. So he made a distinction between those who would get liberation after you die and those who now."
PLAIN: Most ripen slowly, life by life, adding up. A few finish now. The common path leads out at death, not before.

## Beat 5 — Karma-bhumi, only here you act
PRIMARY (Puranic ref: Jambudvipa/Bharata as karma-bhumi; cite, no verbatim needed).
MARK [Karma_and_rebirth.mp3 01:07:30-->01:07:59]: "It's only here if you manage to be reborn in this earth world and especially here in Jambudvipa and Bharat Varsha of Jambudvipa, then it's called Karma Bhoomi. Because in the place of Karma, this is where you can act to create positive Karma."
PLAIN: Only here can you make new karma. Everywhere else you only undergo old results. So the workshop is this world — next: what you work with (Essay 5, seeds).
