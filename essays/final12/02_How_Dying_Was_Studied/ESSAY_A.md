## 4. karma-07 — door (password, handoff)
AUDIO: audio/Karma_and_rebirth.mp3 | 00:33:00,500 --> 00:34:28,489 | transcripts/Karma_and_rebirth.txt + .srt
> "So here the soul is rising. There is one account of what happens when you die. One is that you climb, you go up first to the sun. You rise towards the sun. Then at the sun there is a door. It's called a door, Brahma Dwar. The door to the Brahman. It was later on became this one here, Brahma Dwaran. And you go to the door and you knock on the door. And there is a little voice that you hear from the other side of the door. And he asks, who are you? Who is that? Who are you? Now if you reply Raj or Mark or something, then he says, no, wrong answer. Here, door closed, door closed, you go back, you have to find your way to the other, along the other way. Because of course the right answer is, I am he. You have to say, I am you, I am Brahma Smi. Then the door opens."
PLAIN: Climb to the sun, find the door, knock. Names fail — Raj, Mark, wrong answer, door closed. Say identity, not name: I am you. Then it opens. Rumi knocked the same door. Next: what happens after you go through — travel, burning, numbers (Essay 3).
