# SHORT: The Hook and the Thread (7 min read, 7 clips)

All voice is Mark Dyczkowski verbatim. Brackets are mine for stitching.

He opens with the modern objection, because you have it too:

> "what view they would take on this and what view would be taken by somebody, ultra-modern, who does not accept rebirth. Must Kashmir Shaivism depend on the theory of rebirth?" [karma-01, 00:00:00-->00:00:22]

His answer is not belief. It is procedure. Initiation is extraction first:

> "he imagines that as he exhales in his prana there's a little golden hook and it goes into the nostril of the disciple... it hooks on to the jiva, the individual soul." [karma-10, 01:13:32-->01:14:02]

[The breath is the vehicle. Your teacher breathes out, you breathe in, the hook goes to the heart where breath halts. He inhales and pulls you out.]

Then projection:

> "He would then connect your Prana with his. His Prana would then, he would then exhale onto a thread with five knots and on that thread were projected your new body because your life breath which is what you're made of as an individual soul is now being [exhaled]" [vyoma-04, 00:13:30-->00:14:00]

[That thread is the ladder. Five knots, one jiva at the start.]

Then travel:

> "resonant with that world. Body, senses and so on. There would be experiences and there would be a geography of that world and so on. Then he would say the next mantra and extract you from that world and breathe you into the next one and you'd be reborn in the next one." [vyoma-05, 00:14:30-->00:14:59]

[Each mantra rebirths you with an apt body. Bit of thread burnt at each knot. Karma lived out instead of stored.]

Why it matters where you land:

> "it's only here if you manage to be reborn in this earth world and especially here in Jambudvipa and Bharat Varsha of Jambudvipa, then it's called Karma Bhoomi. Because in the place of Karma, this is where you can act to create positive Karma." [karma-12, 01:07:30-->01:07:59]

[Other worlds you only undergo. Here you can act.]

Why you don't die on the spot when karma is gone:

> "So when you're initiated, the Guru would keep a certain amount of karma he wouldn't remove, it's called Prarabda, this karma which is on the way of working itself out." [vyoma-07, 00:17:24-->00:17:35]

[Enough fuel left to finish the life. Then no return.]

He closes the loop himself, insider and scholar at once:

> "Kashmir Shaivism, like every system in India, has... Samanya Shastra. The teachings that are common to everybody. And then the particular schools have Vishesha." [karma-14, 01:28:30-->01:28:57]

Rebirth is samanya — common to all. What you do with it on a thread is vishesha — this school's tech.
