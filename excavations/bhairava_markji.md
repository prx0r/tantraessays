# Bhairava + Markji — excavation

Sources: `audio/Bhairava_Stava_lecture.mp3` 60.0m + `audio/Bhairava_Stava_recitation.mp3` 6.4m, verses `audio/Bhairava_Stava_verses.pdf`; `audio/Markji_Intro_CIIS_2010.m4a` 36.8m. Transcripts + .srt in `transcripts/`.
Thesis question: what does Abhinavagupta's Bhairava hymn actually teach — breath, fearlessness, devotion — and how does Markji frame that inside the consciousness-philosophy of Kashmir Shaivism?
