## Blunt phrases (use in plain lines, never in quotes)
- stilling of preparations → everything the mind makes comes to a stop
- extinction, not annihilation → fire going out, no one burned
- thread with five knots → ladder you climb by being reborn up it
- golden hook → breath-hook that pulls the soul out
- Karma-bhumi → only here you can act, elsewhere you only undergo
- I am you → identity password, not name
- pure/impure set aside → no one excluded from the room
- knowing is loving → to know him is to love him, same act
