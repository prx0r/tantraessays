# Gita 2.22 + 2.27 — garments + certain death (Arnold 1885, public domain)

Arnold:
> Nay, but as when one layeth / His worn-out robes away, / And, taking new ones, sayeth, / "These will I wear to-day!" / So putteth by the spirit / Lightly its garb of flesh, / And passeth to inherit / A residence afresh.

Gita 2.27 (same ch, for samanya close — Arnold):
> The end of birth is death; the end of death / Is birth: this is ordained!

PLAIN: Body is clothing. Soul changes bodies like clothes. Death ends birth, birth ends death — ordained, not optional. This is the verse every Hindu school agrees on, which is why Mark calls it samanya.

Use: Essay 1 Beat 5. Mark samanya/vishesh (karma-14) presupposes this.
