# Yajnavalkya–Artikl? Artabhaga secret — Brihadaranyaka 3.2.13 (Max Muller SBE, public domain)

Jaratk? Jaratkarava Artabhaga asks Yajnavalkya what happens on death re: good/bad deeds. Text: "one becomes good by good karma, bad by bad karmas... Thereupon Artabhaga held his peace... 'we two alone shall know of this; let this question of ours not be debated in public.' Then these two went out and deliberated. What they said was karman (work), what they praised was karman."

Use: Essay 1 karma-03. Mark: "takes him to one side... secret... controversial... first place doctrine appears."
